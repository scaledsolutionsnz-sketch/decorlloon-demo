About Decorlloon

Southland's balloon stylist for the moments that matter.

Decorlloon is a hands-on balloon styling service based in Invercargill, making garlands, arches and full event setups for celebrations right across Southland. Every job starts with your colours and your space, then gets built by hand into something that fits the room.

Birthdays, weddings, engagements, baby showers or a business launch — the goal is the same: a setup that looks brilliant, holds up all day and takes the stress off your plate.
